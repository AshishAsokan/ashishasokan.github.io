I am a Founding Member of Technical Staff at [Imbik AI](https://imbik.ai/), working on post-training for agents. I completed my MS in Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), advised by [Fernando De la Torre](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~ftorre/) and closely collaborating with [Raviteja Vemulapalli](https://ravitejav.weebly.com/) and [Oncel Tuzel](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Fe7NTe0AAAAJ&hl=en) from Apple MLR. Before that, I was a Predoctoral Fellow at the Indian Institute of Science, advised by [R. Venkatesh Babu](https://cds.iisc.ac.in/faculty/venky/).

My research examines how to rigorously characterize what changes between model versions, and how frontier model capabilities can be transferred to smaller, more accessible models — with the goal of making powerful models more accessible and their development more transparent.
